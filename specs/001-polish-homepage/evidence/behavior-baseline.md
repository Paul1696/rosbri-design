# Preserved behavior baseline

- Shared desktop header: logo, catalogue navigation, search and cart access.
- Mobile header/menu: menu trigger, search, catalogue routes and cart access.
- Hero/customization: modal opening, close control, backdrop/Escape handling, focus management and WhatsApp submission.
- Catalogue cards: product route and add-to-cart action.
- FAQ: accessible accordion state.
- WhatsApp: customization and business quote links retain their existing endpoints.
